# Taste — design / motion

- Wants motion & interaction design that reads as **premium, cinematic, polished and intentional** — smooth, elegant, minimal, on the level of Apple / Awwwards / modern SaaS & creative-agency work, and appropriate to a professional/business context (not gaming-like). Confidence: 0.85
- Explicitly rejects overdone/gimmicky animation: bouncing everything, neon effects, excessive particles, constant movement, oversized cursors, typewriter-everywhere, scroll hijacking, unnecessary sound, or anything that slows the site. Values restraint — "the user should notice the quality of the interaction, not the animation itself." Confidence: 0.8
- Values sophisticated micro-interactions and details as part of polish (magnetic buttons, scroll progress, staggered line-by-line text reveals, parallax, animated nav underlines, image hover states). Confidence: 0.75
- Prefers a restrained, system-like cursor over a showy one: asked for a different cursor style and settled on a small monochrome dot + thin ring with precise tracking and only subtle hover feedback — explicitly dropping the accent-coloured expansion and the floating contextual text label, and later cutting the smoothed/trailing follow so the cursor sits exactly on the pointer each frame ("moves lazy… make it ok as normal"). Confidence: 0.85
- Wants pointer-driven interactions to respond with native-level immediacy: no lerp/smoothing or trailing "catch-up" on position (only scale/colour/opacity may animate), because laggy following motion reads as broken rather than premium. Confidence: 0.8
- Wants a deliberate mobile animation strategy rather than shrunk-down desktop effects: reduce/disable parallax and decorative motion on mobile, keep scroll reveals and button feedback, and never rely on hover-dependent functionality for touch. Confidence: 0.7
